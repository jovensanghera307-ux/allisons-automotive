Services

What We Fix & Build

Complete mechanical, electrical, and body repair for all makes and models — plus custom paint and mods for cars, trucks, and bikes.

Brakes & ABS Systems

Squealing, grinding, soft pedal, or a dashboard full of warning lights — we handle complete brake service: pads, rotors, calipers, brake lines, and fluid. We also properly diagnose and repair anti-lock brake (ABS) modules and sensors, instead of just guessing at parts.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Stalling, rough idle, misfires, overheating, or a check-engine light that won't go away. We run real diagnostics to find the root cause, then give you an honest assessment of what needs fixing now and what can wait — from tune-ups to major engine work.

Collision & Body Work

Been in an accident? Don't wait two weeks just to get an estimate. We take a look fast, walk you through the repair, and get your vehicle looking and driving right again with quick turnaround. We also do custom body work — kits, fabrication, and cleanup of someone else's bad bodywork.

Electrical Systems

Dead batteries, faulty alternators, wiring problems, sensor faults, and mystery warning lights. Modern vehicles are packed with electronics — we have the tools and know-how to trace electrical problems other shops can't figure out.

Steering & Suspension

Clunks over bumps, wandering steering, uneven tire wear, or a rough ride. We service shocks, struts, ball joints, tie rods, and wheel bearings to keep your car planted and safe.

Routine Maintenance

Oil changes, fluid services, filters, belts, hoses, and scheduled factory maintenance. Regular care from a shop you trust is the cheapest insurance your vehicle can get.

Heating & A/C

No heat in January or no cold air in July? We diagnose and repair heating and air conditioning systems so you're comfortable year-round.

Allison's KustomZ

Custom Work

Repair keeps you on the road. Custom work makes the road yours. Bring us your daily driver, your project car, or your bike.

Custom Paint & Resprays

Full resprays, color changes, custom colors, and finish work for cars and trucks. From factory-fresh to one-of-one, we lay down paint that turns heads and holds up.

Motorcycle & Bike Painting

Tanks, fairings, fenders, frames — custom paint for motorcycles and bikes. Your ride should look as good parked as it does moving.

Mods & Customization

Appearance and performance modifications, body kits, and custom fabrication. Tell us what you're picturing and we'll build it — clean work, no shortcuts.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need an appointment?
Calling ahead at (302) 565-8108 is the best way to get scheduled quickly. We're open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM, and we work hard to get vehicles in fast — often much sooner than other shops in the area.
Do you work on all makes and models?
Yes — we service both domestic and import vehicles, from routine maintenance to complex diagnostics.
Do you handle body work after an accident?
Yes. We handle collision and body repair, and unlike many shops we won't make you wait weeks just to have your vehicle looked at.
How do estimates work?
We diagnose the problem first, explain what's wrong in plain language, and give you an honest price before any work begins. No surprise charges.
My warning lights are on — what should I do?
Don't panic, but don't ignore it either. Give us a call and describe what you're seeing — we'll tell you whether it's safe to drive in or whether you should have the vehicle towed.
Do you paint motorcycles and bikes?
Yes — custom paint for tanks, fairings, fenders, and frames is one of our specialties. Bring the bike or just the parts, and tell us what you're picturing.
Can you customize my car?
Absolutely. Custom paint, body kits, appearance and performance mods, and custom fabrication — from a single upgrade to a full build. Call us to talk through your project.

Not Sure What's Wrong?

Describe the symptoms and we'll point you in the right direction — no obligation.
