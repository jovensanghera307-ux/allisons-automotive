Our Story

The Shop That Answers the Phone

Allison's Automotive was built around a simple frustration every driver knows: calling shop after shop, getting vague answers, long waits, and prices that change once the car is on the lift.

We do it differently. When you call, you talk to someone who actually knows cars. When you drop off your vehicle, you get an honest diagnosis and a real price. And when we say it'll be ready, it's ready. That's why customers who called five other shops end up staying with us for good.

From routine maintenance to tricky electrical diagnostics and post-accident body work, we treat every vehicle like it's our own — and every customer like a neighbor.
